DeepSeek does not charge extra to store the prefix. The first visit pays for fresh input. The next visit, if the prefix matches completely, pays the hit price. There is no guarantee. It is a best-effort system. Anthropic uses a different rule. Storing a five-minute copy costs twenty-five percent more than ordinary input. Reading that copy costs one tenth. Under that rule, the second visit already pays it back. In both systems, the output is paid in full.
