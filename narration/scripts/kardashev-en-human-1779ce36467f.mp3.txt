You are looking at Earth, with real NASA imagery mapped onto the globe. The Kardashev scale compares the power a civilization uses: how much energy it can harness every second. We start from a rounded reference of twenty terawatts, not a measurement for today. The index is about zero point seven three. That sounds close to one, but it represents only zero point two percent of Type One power. It would still need to grow five hundred times.
