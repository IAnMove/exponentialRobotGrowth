An assembled robot still needs verification, calibration and commissioning. This is the final step before we count it as finished and allow it to join the workforce. Watch the bodies arriving and the rate at which they leave. If testing falls behind, faster assembly builds a backlog without expanding the available fleet as quickly. Reinforcing this stage can release that waiting production. The scene simplifies the process and does not simulate rejected units or random breakdowns. It still preserves an essential distinction: making components, assembling a robot and having an operational robot are different results.
