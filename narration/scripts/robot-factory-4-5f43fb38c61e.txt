This is the final station. Completing it increases the number of robots built and makes a robot available to deploy. In automatic mode, the factory sends it to a station that still has a human task and low daily capacity. Deployment takes a short simulated time. Follow that robot as it returns to the line. Some of what the factory produced is now helping produce the next units. Once all ten tasks are covered, additional robots remain available outside the line. Machinery and supply do not multiply themselves. That is why we explore the district and the region next.
