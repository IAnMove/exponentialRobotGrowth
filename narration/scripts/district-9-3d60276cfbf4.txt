This neighborhood lets you follow people after they leave the industrial line. In the scenario, they rest at night and return when their shift begins. When a robot takes over a task, the person remains in the world and moves to the neighborhood or park. This represents tasks being covered, not a prediction about employment. We use one human shift to make breaks visible, although a real factory can organize several shifts. The contrast helps explain how equipment operating time changes, alongside the number of available workers.
