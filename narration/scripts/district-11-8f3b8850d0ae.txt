Robots stop too. In this example, each robot reserves two hours for charging and one for maintenance, leaving twenty-one hours available for tasks. Schedules are staggered so some robots can continue working while others are here. Watch those leaving their stations and returning to industry. A factory operating around the clock does not mean every individual robot works twenty-four hours without a break. Energy also comes from outside. As the fleet grows, electricity, chargers, spare parts and maintenance capacity must be available. Expansion requires all of this supporting infrastructure.
