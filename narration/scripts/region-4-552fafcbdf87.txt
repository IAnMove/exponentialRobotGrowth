The panels represent new generation facilities connected to the grid. Each adds electrical capacity per cycle. This is not a battery or a count of stored energy. Demand comes from operating workstations, buildings and construction. If demand exceeds available capacity, the model reduces both industrial output and construction speed. A new power facility does not directly produce robots, but it can allow many other facilities to use their capacity. This dependency shows why expanding the robot fleet also requires expanding its infrastructure. Weather, real power grids and specialized electrical equipment inputs are not simulated here.
