# Kardashev asset credits

- `marco.glb`: NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD),
  [MarCO 3D Model](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/marco-3d-model/).
  Original binary model, 3,992 triangles; rendered at an enlarged display scale.
  [Original download](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/solar/2023/09/m/MarCO.glb).
- `earth-blue-marble.jpg`: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation,
  December 2004, 5400 × 2700 base map.
  [Source and downloads](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/).
- NASA assets are used for educational illustration with attribution under the
  [NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
  No NASA endorsement is implied. MarCO's actual mission travelled toward Mars;
  the Earth scene places enlarged copies illustratively, not on mission trajectories.
- Dyson collector assemblies, solar surface shader and spiral galaxy are original
  procedural illustrations. They are not validated engineering designs or observations
  of extraterrestrial civilizations. Quantities and distances are not to scale.
- Three.js and its GLTFLoader, BufferGeometryUtils, SkeletonUtils and RoomEnvironment
  modules: MIT license, copied from the locked dependency to `vendor/` during the build.
- Ten original bilingual narration scripts are recorded with MiniMax `speech-2.8-hd`.
  The public `voices.js` includes transcripts, durations and local MP3 references.
  No external API is called during playback and no credentials are shipped.
