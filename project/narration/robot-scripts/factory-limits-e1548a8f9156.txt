With automatic handover and the current assumptions, robots built by this line can cover its ten tasks: two per station. Check the counter as several simulated days pass. If you disable automatic handover, the outcome depends on your chosen deployments and human positions may remain. Once all ten tasks are covered, additional robots stay in output stock; they do not add workstations or also work in another factory. The number manufactured can keep rising, but this line's workforce no longer grows. We start with one hundred and sixty kits, with up to one hundred and sixty new kits arriving each day and limited storage space. Machinery and those supplies constrain output. Cumulative production can rise while the daily rate levels off: that is not sustained exponential growth. Accelerating again would require more capacity and a supply chain able to support it.
