At this station, people or robots assemble the frame supporting the torso and limbs. Observe the product sample, its identifier and work progress. The same identifier connects a part to its steps through the line. Figures at each station are samples of the process; queues and counters show the actual model quantities. An assembled frame is not an operational robot yet: actuators, a battery, calibration and testing are still needed. Automating this station while another is slower can fill a queue without finishing many additional robots. Automatic mode reinforces stations with lower daily capacity. You can turn it off to decide where to send a robot the line itself has already finished.
