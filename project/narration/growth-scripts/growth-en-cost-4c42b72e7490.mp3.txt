Finally, compare the cost of the same unit. Human cost adds material and labor. Robot cost adds material and spreads installation, service, supervision and electricity across active hours. Change the assumptions: savings can grow, shrink or disappear. Adding identical robots does not automatically lower each unit’s cost; higher utilization spreads fixed costs better. These euros do not finance the earlier simulation. Shared costs and financing are omitted, and no value is a commercial price. The aim is to understand growth, available goods and cost separately.
