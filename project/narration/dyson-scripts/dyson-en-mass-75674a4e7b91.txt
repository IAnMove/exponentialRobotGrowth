Distance also changes the required area. Keep the same effective coverage and double the radius: the ideal area grows fourfold. With the same mass per square metre, total mass also increases by four. Surface density is an editable assumption, not a measured property of an available collector. This calculation counts only the ideal collecting surface. It excludes supports, manufacturing, payloads, transport, maintenance and shadows. The other side shows a separate historical comparison: spreading Jupiter's entire mass over a sphere at the selected radius. The resulting column and thickness illustrate a scale of material. They do not require dismantling Jupiter, and we are not simulating that process. Check how radius, flux, reference temperature, area and mass relate before visiting the shell.
