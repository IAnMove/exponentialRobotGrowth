The wafer enters the exposure scanner. A mask carries the pattern and the optics project it onto the resist. This model does not reproduce a commercial scanner or its full optical system. Look at the three highlighted regions: light changes their state, while the film underneath remains intact. The wafer then returns to the developing tool.
