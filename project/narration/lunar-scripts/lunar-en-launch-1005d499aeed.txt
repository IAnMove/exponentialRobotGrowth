Every delivery has to reach the lunar surface. Launching into orbit is not the same as landing on the Moon: it takes a transfer trajectory, maneuvers, descent and possibly other support flights. Here we count useful cargo delivered, without modeling fuel or refueling. At twenty-five tonnes per delivery, the one-hundred-tonne seed requires four initial deliveries. The frequency control then sets how many additional loads arrive each year. Vehicles and distances are schematic.
