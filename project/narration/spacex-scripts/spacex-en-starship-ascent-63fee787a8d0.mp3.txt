Super Heavy begins its ascent with thirty-three engines. The ship and example payload remain attached to the booster. Now watch hot staging. Ship engines ignite while the booster still retains thrust with an engine subset. The ring between the stages vents that exhaust. The stages then separate physically. The ship continues upward; the booster turns and performs a boostback burn to direct its path towards the tower. These are simultaneous jobs that you can follow separately with the camera buttons. After boostback ends, the booster keeps moving with its engines off. The trajectories are explanatory curves rather than integrated forces or predictions of altitude and velocity. Position one hundred on the bar does not mean one hundred real seconds either.
