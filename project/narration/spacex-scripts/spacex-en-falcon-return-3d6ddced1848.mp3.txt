We now have two trajectories. Follow the booster. Choose droneship to watch it continue downrange towards a floating platform. Choose coast to see the path that changed direction earlier during a return burn. Not every droneship landing needs that burn. Engines stay off between manoeuvres: falling does not mean firing. The entry burn reduces speed and grid fins help orient the stage in the air. Near its destination, four legs deploy, the landing burn begins and their feet reach the platform. Flames turn off at completion. Return plumes represent an engine subset; we are not calculating the firing programme of a real flight. The expendable profile has no recovery manoeuvres or recoverable landing. Meanwhile, the upper stage retains its path with the payload.
