Follow the booster descending towards the tower. This time we do not deploy four legs or add a droneship. Super Heavy orients its body and performs a final burn to reduce speed. The arms move beneath support points near the upper end of the stage. Descent finishes when those supports reach the arms, and thrust shuts down. This shows what catching a booster means: receiving hardware with a structure rather than magically stopping a rocket still far away. A few plumes represent the manoeuvre; exact engine counts depend on vehicle and flight. Booster catches have been demonstrated in tests. That alone does not demonstrate routine recovery of the upper ship or mean both stages are instantly ready to fly again. The ship continues its separate journey as we inspect this contact.
